# BOM notes

Prices are indicative concept estimates (TRL 2) in US dollars for one prototype and will be confirmed with named suppliers at TRL 3. Item numbers 1 to 11 match the exploded view (`media/exploded.png`); items 12 and 13 have no callout.

| Group | Items | Indicative cost |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Frame, clusters and drive | 1 to 4 | about $295 |
| Electronics and controls | 5, 6, 8, 9, 13 | about $120 |
| Battery and charger | 7, 12 | about $145 |
| Load strap and skids | 10, 11 | about $20 |
| **Total** | 1 to 13 | **about $580** |

The total is about 3 % under the $600 budget in `project.yaml` and requirement R12. Options in SCM-PRC-001 that add a telescoping handle or a tilting load platform would take it over budget; they are proposed, awaiting Amish, and are not in this BOM.

Shipping, taxes and tools are not included.
